# U68 to two resident 290B formatted fields

`pr_u68_290b_two_start` and `pr_u68_290b_two_resume` compose the existing [U68-to-first-field path](RES_290B_FIRST_FIELD.md) directly into `pr_290b_second_field`. The latter starts from the live `290B:+29A6` frame and stops at `+2A02`, before the next record-dependent branch. It reloads `DS:[49D2]`, multiplies the live word at record `+0190` by ten into DX:AX, reloads the pointer and adds the live word at `+018E` to AX modulo 65536. It formats the resulting low word through the real `00BA:1073` and `0B25`; a one-digit result follows the source's caller-supplied `CS:28F2` literal through real `0B0B`, `0B98` and `0B25`. The first field and completed upstream state remain on the shared bus. Neither field is displayed by this slice.

Source binding: owner START.EXE SHA-256 `d825df2b174675c9088ba1489488bdeebe66ad2a22943f17d3a198e60b6a07bd`; loader-expanded `00BA:[29A6,2A02)` is 92 bytes, SHA-256 `c280b3f9f6b6af89e6199e84eab8da5084ac9f6f0d8c837f25b9d97f6af2a0f5`. The span and calls were decoded independently; the prior [290B audit](RESIDENT_ROUTE_290B.md) identified the two live word reads and modulo arithmetic. No game bytes or private literal are embedded.

The caller owns the actual first-field frame, loaded code/relocations, live record and private literal, bus/EGA and all services under the first-field and U68 contracts. A standalone second-field entry requires IP `29A6`, BP `[043C,FFEE]`, `SP=BP-0232h`, exactly 1 MiB RAM with EGA attached, and ordinary CS/DS/SS windows below `9000` or at least `B000`. Each reached formatter or flat string leaf retains the existing live segment guards and source-call frames. Both record words are read through the shared bus. If either effective word offset is `FFFF`, the wrapper stops before that read at `+29B3` or `+29BC` respectively. The latter stop retains the actual multiply result and pinned normal/386 MULW flag convention: SF/AF preserved, ZF from AX, PF from all four product bytes, and CF/OF from nonzero DX; the subsequent ADD normally overwrites those flags. This is a bounded profile, not a general alias or hardware proof.

Malformed calls stop after earlier pushes/effects. Nested formatter and string stops, transfers and EGA profile failures retain CPU, RAM and EGA state and are exposed through diagnostics. The composed bridge propagates only its own idle yield and never retries a terminal stop. `TWO_FIELDS` means only that `+2A02` was reached; the later record branch, remaining 290B string construction, display, U68 return, saved gameplay, native/visual parity and coverage credit are not claimed.

Synthetic tests compose real U68 idle-to-Up, both fields, actual formatter/string leaves and FIFO on one bus. They cover one-/multi-digit fields, 16-bit multiplication/addition wrap, live record/stack alias, EGA word reads and latches, both unsupported word-wrap stops including intermediate MUL flags, malformed call after arithmetic, altered suspended CPU and invalid-frame atomicity. The wrapper and fixture passed strict-warning ASan/UBSan linked against the existing provider archive. These synthetic tests are not an original-runtime or native differential for this composition.
