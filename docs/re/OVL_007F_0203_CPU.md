# OVL_007F +0203: translated, evidenced capped add with a message, composed over +0054, +003A, StrLoad and resident 1693 (evidence only; no credit proposed)

This raw CPU body, `OVL_007F_0203` (unit 0x7F, offset 0203h, no entry slot), takes one word AMOUNT and a record addressed by the far pointer at `DS:[5D92]`. It asks [`OVL_007F_0054`](OVL_007F_0054_CPU.md) whether adding AMOUNT to the record's word at `+0102h`
would exceed the class limit V (the `+0000` value). If it would, the body **shows a message through resident `00BA:1693`**, adds only the room that was left (V − W) to the record's accumulator word at `+0090h` and to `+0102h` (through `+003A`), and adds the rest of AMOUNT
to a 32-bit counter at `DS:[6804]`. If it would not, it adds all of AMOUNT to `+0090h` and calls `+003A` with it. This change adds the model, its checker, a shared world harness for the bodies that call 1693, the native harness for the no-display branch and this document. It proposes **no strict row,
no identity row and no manifest row**; par-e accepts or refuses.

## What the body does

Frame after the call: `[SP]` IP, `[SP+2]` CS, `[SP+4]` AMOUNT. `RETF 2`. Locals: the 42-byte string temporary at `[BP-2Ah..BP-1]` and the excess word at `[BP-2]`.

1. `PUSH BP; MOV BP,SP; SUB SP,2Ah`. `+0054(record = G, extra = AMOUNT, out = &[BP-2])`; `OR AL,AL; JE 0273`.
2. **Above** (AL nonzero): `StrLoad(&[BP-2Ah], CS:01DB)` leaves the string temporary and its pointer on the stack; `LCALL 00BA:1693` shows it (clear row, print, delay, clear row). `[G+90h] += [BP-2]` (through `LES DI,[5D92]`);
   `+003A(G, [BP-2])`; the 32-bit counter `DS:[6806:6804]` := counter + AMOUNT − [BP-2] (`ADD/ADC/SUB/SBB`); `JMP 028E`.
3. **Not above**: `[G+90h] += AMOUNT`; `+003A(G, AMOUNT)`.
4. `MOV SP,BP; POP BP; RETF 2`.

## Role (identity is not claimed)

The role is a **capped add into a record with an overflow message**: it fills the record's `+0102h` word up to the class limit, puts the overflow into a 32-bit counter, and shows a message once. Measured ownership: **two near callers inside unit 0x7F, both in the body at 19B2** (sites 1ED4 and 2227), no fixup-backed caller. This note makes no claim about identity or about what `+0090h`, `+0102h` or the counter mean:
the two-factor test is the reviewer's.

**Where the exposure lives:** the body's own aperture-reachable access is the one `[G+90h]` read-modify-write, through the bus. The record's other words are reached by callees whose contracts differ: `+0054` reads `+102h` through the bus, `+0000`/13AB read `+10h` and `+16h` flat, and `+003A` reads and writes `+102h` flat. **A G aimed into the aperture therefore splits the record across three conventions**; that is the declared contract limit, shared with `OVL_007F_0054_CPU.md`, and no differential case places G there.

### What pins the bus claim

The accumulator read-modify-write is pinned by the **C test, which attaches an EGA**: G in the aperture, plane 0 holding `34h`/`12h` at `+90h`/`+91h`, the not-above branch writes `34h`/`13h` to **all four planes** and leaves the RAM bytes under the aperture untouched; the same state without an EGA is flat RAM.
The two branches share one helper (`acc_add`), so the above branch's read-modify-write is the same code; **the mutant that bypasses the bus there is caught by that C test**. This is stated as a code-sharing fact, not as an above-branch run: the C test cannot run the above branch (it needs the 1693 closure's whole world). The differential cannot see a bypass, because G is not in the aperture in it.

- **`g90-at-bp-34 .. +0f` (2 x 64 cases, above and not-above)**: `[G+90h]` on each byte of the body's frame from `BP-34h` to `BP+0Fh`: **the 42-byte string temporary** (StrLoad writes it, 1693 reads it, so the read-modify-write can change the message 1693 shows), **the excess local** (so the excess changes between `+0054` and the add), the saved BP, the return address (TRANSFER) and AMOUNT.

### Mutation

**17 mutants: 16 caught, 15 by the Unicorn differential and 1 by the C test; 1 equivalent.** Caught by the differential: JE inverted, the message at 01DCh (the string then loaded is not the original's), the accumulator at +92h, ADC without the carry (caught by `ctr-ffff-0000-7000`), SBB without the borrow, the counter's low word stored at 6806, `+003A` given AMOUNT instead of the excess, RETF popping 4, `SUB SP,2Ch`, `+0054` given the word at `[BP+8]`, the excess local at BP-4, **the return-address (TRANSFER) check dropped (caught by `g90-at-bp+02-above`)**, the `+0054` call-site displacement, the `+0054` call and the 1693 call not charged to the budget.
**The mutant that makes the accumulator read-modify-write bypass the bus survives the differential, as predicted, and is caught by the C test** (G in the aperture; one helper serves both branches). The differential cannot see it: G is not in the aperture in any differential case.
**One mutant is equivalent: `OR AL,AL` testing 16 bits instead of 8.** The two differ only in SF, and only when AL has bit 7 set at 021D. AL is the byte `+0054` returns, which `+0054` sets to 0 or 1 in its own frame and reads back at its `009F`; the only store `+0054` makes is through its out pointer, which is `[BP-2]` of **this** body's frame, a different and higher address than `+0054`'s flag byte. So AL is 0 or 1 whatever G holds, SF is always clear, and the two tests agree. It survived the 36-case mutation set; the proof, not the survival, is the claim. A first version of the bypass mutant did not compile (an unused helper under `-Werror`) and was rewritten to keep the helpers referenced; a compile error is not counted as a catch.

### Declared environment and substitutions

1. There are none that stand in for original behaviour: no stub, no INT 3Fh dispatch, no environment service. The whole composition runs as original bytes in the oracle.
2. **Scope of the 1693 closure:** it runs with generous loop-back limits and no refused OUT, so its own LIMIT, refusal and CODE stops are not exercised here.
3. **A new shared module, `ovl_007f_world1693.py`,** places the unit-0x7F spans, the 13AB closure and StrLoad into the 1693 audit's world and runs the oracle and the C side; it imports the 1693 audit's world builder and EGA device unchanged. The oracle handles **near `RET`** (the delay helpers use them): my first version popped only far returns, and the first delay case reported a false TRANSFER; that was the oracle, not the model.

### Which way verdicts move under a convention split

- **Word accesses that straddle FFFFh:** two placements (`g-off-ff6f`, `g-off-fefd`) put a record word at offset FFFFh; the corpus and Unicorn read the second byte at the next physical address, and the pinned DOSBox-X build is recorded as wrapping it. **Those two placements (four cases) are the only ones that depend on it**; the packaged DOSBox-X did not show the split in the `+0054` probes. Not run on the pinned build.
- **A G in the aperture:** the record would be split across the three conventions above; the verdict moves from reproducible to not on a machine whose plane reads differ from flat bytes. No differential case places G there.
