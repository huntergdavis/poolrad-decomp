# Unit 0x3B4 +0x0000 (ledger slot 2): coordinate stage on the movement path, whole-routine translation

Status: whole-routine C translation of the routine's effects, with two external callees as hooks, checked by a focused behaviour test, a
static decode audit, and a differential oracle against the original bytes (the external callees are stubs). Translation only. No game run,
no live-memory check, no caller integration, no parity, function-identity or credit claim.

## Claim, exactly

`pr_u3b4_entry2_0000` (src/recovered/overlay_u3b4_entry2_0000.c) performs, in the file's order: the table lookup from DS:0x49FA; the record
add and the two signed and unsigned byte mappings from the record words at +0x186 and +0x188; the five argument words of the call to
`0x3E9:0x39`, including the division steps on DS:0x6DDC; the update of DS:0x6DDC modulo 6; the call to `0x464:0x1B52`; and the return. The two
callees are hooks; the translation makes no claim about what they do. Claim boundary: the routine's own effects, given the callee behaviour
the caller supplies.

Not claimed: the callee behaviour; that the callees are the routines named below in the live game; the meaning of the two mapped bytes; the
content of anything the callees draw or store; live operand values (no game run); caller integration (the view refresh at unit +0x016D calls
this entry with a near call, and that call is not modelled here); credit on the function backlog.

## ABI facts fixed from the bytes

1. **AX is a full 16-bit register and its entry value is never read.** The first instructions (`mov al,[0x49FA] ; xor ah,ah`) overwrite AH; the
   entry AL is overwritten by the same MOV AL.
2. **The record add carries into AH.** `add ax,es:[di+0x186]` is a full-width add. After it, the high byte H of AX is fixed for the rest of the
   routine's pushes (the MOV AL and the later instructions do not change AH until the division steps). The first three words are (H : byte).
3. **Signed mapping of the first byte.** `[bp-1]` is the low byte of the sum. A value of 2 or more (signed) is mapped: below 0x2A becomes 2,
   exactly 0x2A becomes 3, and 0x2B or more becomes 4. A value below 2 (signed; negative values such as 0x80 included) is kept.
4. **Unsigned mapping of the second byte.** The word at +0x188 is compared unsigned: below 2 gives its low byte; 2 up to 0x21 gives 2; 0x22
   gives 3; 0x23 or more gives 4.
5. **Division steps.** `cdq ; mov cx,2 ; idiv cx` on the byte from DS:0x6DDC (AH zero): w4 is the remainder (byte mod 2). `shr ax,1 ; add ax,9`
   gives w5 = (byte >> 1) + 9. After the callee, `cdq ; mov cx,6 ; idiv cx` on (byte + 1) gives the remainder, stored to DS:0x6DDC. The byte is
   read before the callee for w4 and w5, and after it for the update.
6. **Five words**, pushed in this order: w1 (the first byte with H), w2 (the second byte with H), w3 (DS:0x6AAF with H), w4, w5. The callee
   returns with RETF 0xA, so the five words are its arguments.
7. **Frame and registers.** `push bp ; mov bp,sp ; sub sp,2` reserves two local bytes. BP and SP are restored by `mov sp,bp ; pop bp ; retf`.
   DS is not reloaded. ES is set by each LES (from DS:0x49D2) and is not restored.
8. **Exit AX and DX are not modelled.** They depend on the last division and the last callee path.

## Checks

1. **Static decode audit**, tests/overlay/ovl3b4_0000_static_test.py, against the owner GAME.OVR (read at run time, not committed): 27 of 27
   PASS (span length and SHA-256; 72 instructions tile the span; one plain RETF last; the two external calls; the record, table, clamp, band,
   record-word, DS:0x6AAF and DS:0x6DDC instructions by address and operand text; the two CDQ and two IDIV CX instructions).
2. **Focused behaviour check**, tests/recovered/test_overlay_u3b4_entry2_0000.c (ctest `poolrad_overlay_u3b4_entry2_0000_tests`): compiled with
   `-std=c11 -Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic`, PASS. It checks the guards; the basic words and the update (0x0002, 0x0000, 0x0007, 0x0001,
   0x000E, and DS:0x6DDC from 0x0B to 0x00); the AH carry from the record add (0x1000, 0x1004, 0x10FF); the signed clamp of a negative first
   byte (0x80 kept); the bands at 0x2A, 0x2B, 0x29 and the second-byte bands at 0x21, 0x22; and a far read failure that stops the routine
   before either call.
3. **Differential oracle**, tests/overlay/ovl3b4_0000_oracle.py: the 199 owner bytes run under Unicorn 2.1.4 (16-bit real mode) at CS 0x03CA.
   The two external calls go to stubs that log the pushed words, apply the case's DS writes, and pop their arguments with RETF. The same cases
   run through the C translation via tests/overlay/ovl3b4_0000_harness.c. Per case: the call log, a status that requires SP restored to
   entry+4, BP restored, and each stub entry to see the stack the routine implies, and the DS bytes that differ from their initial values.
   - Seed 1, 400 cases: 400 of 400 equal; stub arity mismatches 0. Executed in emulation: the first clamp applied in 200 cases; the second band
     (0x2A or more) in 140; the record byte below 2 in 101; the record word at 0x22 or more in 193; both calls in all 400.
   - Seed 2, 2000 cases: 2000 of 2000 equal; stub arity mismatches 0. The first clamp applied in 994 cases; the second band in 662; the record
     byte below 2 in 489; the record word at 0x22 or more in 991; both calls in all 2000. Mutants killed: five-word call without +9, 2000 of
     2000; quotient not remainder, 1985 of 2000; top band always 3, 730 of 2000; second word's AH dropped, 458 of 2000; modulo 5 update, 1548
     of 2000; first byte compared unsigned, 993 of 2000.
   - Mutants (one edit each to a copy of the translation; each must differ from the original bytes), seed 1:
     | Mutant | cases that differ (of 400) |
     | --- | --- |
     | the five-word call's fifth word without the +9 | 400 |
     | the fourth word is the quotient, not the remainder | 397 |
     | the second byte's top band always 3 | 138 |
     | the second word's AH dropped | 106 |

## Limits

- **No game run.** The callee identities and live operand values are not checked. The meaning of the two mapped bytes is not claimed.
- **Oracle callees are stubs.** The stubs apply the case's DS writes and assume the RETF conventions above.
- **Callees unresolved on main**: `0x3E9:0x39` (unit 0x3CE entry slot 5) and `0x464:0x1B52` (resident). They are hooks with no behavioural claim.
- **Exit AX and DX are not modelled.** ES is set by each LES and is not restored (see ABI fact 7).
- **Caller integration** (the view refresh's near call on flags 2 to 4) is not checked; the view refresh translation's near-call hook for this entry is not wired here.
- **16-bit wrap** of record and DS offsets is not oracle-checked: the oracle keeps record offsets below 0xF000 and table offsets within DS.
- **Authorship.** The commits carry the project's placeholder identity; re-attribute before any public release.
