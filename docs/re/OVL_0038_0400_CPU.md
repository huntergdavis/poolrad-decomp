# OVL_0038_0400: the 009E dispatcher's slot-11 handler (raw CPU, composing 23DB)

`pr_u38_handler_0400_cpu` is a raw-CPU, full-state model of GAME.OVR unit `38h` `+0400`. The `009E` dispatcher reaches it
through slot 11 (`DS:6854`, stub `0041:0057`), which the credited installer `OVL_0038_31A2` stores. It runs on flat RAM, as the
dispatcher's handlers do, and composes the merged model of `RES_00BA_23DB` (the party count).

**Claim.** PR #1548 supplied the full-state body without granting identity or credit.
The separate non-author t83 grant below records the accepted two-factor identity
and canonical strict row under the PM dispatch-slot ruling. **Slot 11 is not
flipped** in `overlay_u9e_dispatch_0000_cpu.c` in this change: `u9e_dispatch_0000_audit.py` derives a slot's kind from
`strict_full_c_addresses.csv`, so the `K_RAW` flip follows the credit, in a separate PR (agreed with par-c).

## Behaviour

Arguments: `[BP+6]` a selector byte, `[BP+8]` far pointer A, `[BP+0Ch]` far pointer B. Every far pointer is reread with
`LES` at each use.

- **Selector non-zero:** `B+10Eh` := bit 6 of `A+3` (`AND 40h`, `SHR 6`). When `B+84h` is `B3h`, it becomes 0. Return.
- **Selector zero, `A+3` bit 5 set:** `DS:6825` := `64h`. Return.
- **Selector zero, `A+3` bit 5 clear:**
  1. `A+3` := low byte of `A+3 + 20h + (CBW(B+10Eh) << 6)`;
  2. `B+10Eh` := new `A+3` >> 7; `B+10Fh` := 1;
  3. when `B+84h` ≤ `7Fh` (unsigned), `B+84h` := `B3h`;
  4. the far pointer at `B+108h` is followed and its words `+0Ah` and `+0Ch` become 0;
  5. `00BA:23DB` counts the party into `DS:6814`/`6815` (indexed by each counted character's `+10Eh`);
  6. `DS:6825` := `64h`.

**Statuses.** `OK`, `ARGUMENT` (null pointers, RAM not 1 MiB, IP not `0400h`; nothing changed), `CALLEE` (23DB stopped;
its status in `*callee_status`, for example `LIMIT` on a cyclic party list) and `TRANSFER`. There is no backward jump.
23DB's cap is `party_budget`, in instructions; when slot 11 is wired, the dispatcher will pass its `loopbacks` limit here
(as slot 25 does for 2199).

## Claim table

These are facts from the original bytes. The non-author acceptance below applies the PM's identity ruling.

- **Factor 1.** The only caller is the `009E` dispatcher's table: slot 11 (`DS:6854`), stub `0041:0057`, installed by the
  credited `OVL_0038_31A2` ([OVL_009E_0000_HANDLERS.md](OVL_009E_0000_HANDLERS.md)). It depends on the VP's
  dispatch-slot ruling. The queue lists no direct far or near caller.
- **Factor 2, what the body itself touches.**
  - **`B+10Eh`:** the per-character byte that `RES_00BA_23DB` (credited) uses to index its party count. This body writes
    it, then calls 23DB, which counts the party by that same byte. So B is a party-character record.
  - **`B+84h`:** set to `B3h` or cleared to 0. **`B+10Fh`:** set to 1. **`B+108h`:** a far pointer whose words
    `+0Ah`/`+0Ch` are cleared.
  - **`DS:6825`:** set to `64h` on both selector-zero paths.

## Validation

**Focused C test.** `test_overlay_u38_handler_0400_cpu.c` passes **19** hand-predicted checks, built with asserts on (no
`CMAKE_BUILD_TYPE`, no `NDEBUG`), on synthetic RAM with no game bytes. It covers:

- both selector paths, and a selector whose high byte is set;
- the bit-5 early exit;
- the fold with B+10Eh 1 and FFh, and the B+84h threshold;
- 23DB counting a two-character party;
- a cyclic party list stopping 23DB with `LIMIT`, reported as `CALLEE`;
- the argument checks.

**Unicorn differential.** `tools/overlay/ovl038_0400_cpu_audit.py --unicorn` runs the body and `RES_00BA_23DB` from the
original bytes: the loader-expanded START.EXE at base `1000h` with its relocations, and unit `38h` at its own segment with
its fixups. It compares all 14 CPU words and the whole 1 MiB, with **no mask**; any interrupt or port access fails a case.
Each case's world (records, the party list, the DS bytes, the fill byte and the register seeds) is seeded from the case
name.

**Factor 2: PASS on the body itself.** The body writes the character record's
`+10E` at `041B` and `0478`, after loading B from `[BP+0C]`; it also writes
`+10F` at `0480`. Its composed strict `RES_00BA_23DB` reads that same
character field and counts into `DS:6814 + sx(record[+10E])`.
[RESIDENT_PARTY_COUNT.md](RESIDENT_PARTY_COUNT.md) defines the party list and
record field. The caller frames identified in t82 pass the character far
pointer from `DS:6826/6828` as B and a sidecar node as A. Thus the subject is
a documented character record, not merely the fact that it calls 23DB.
