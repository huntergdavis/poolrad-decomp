# Sixth synthetic COMPARE loader entry

`pr_u1c_compare_loader_entry` implements the U1C `+0126` COMPARE prologue through the relocated two-operand loader stub on the shared bus. It preserves AH while setting AL to 2, models the `SUB SP,0204h` arithmetic flags, pushes the live frame and argument, checks the reached far call and stub, and stops at the loaded U25 `+0000` entry. Invalid initial profiles are rejected before mutation; malformed calls and stubs stop at the reached boundary after prior effects. Segments must satisfy the ordinary-memory profile, and the caller must supply immutable valid code outside EGA and a nonwrapping stack frame. The helper requires entry SP in `[0436,FFFB]`, matching the conservative candidate profile.

`pr_sixth_compare_start` and `pr_sixth_compare_resume` compose the [sixth dispatch](U1C_SIXTH_DISPATCH_SHARED_BUS.md) into this entry only when the reached handler is COMPARE `+0126`. Other handler entries and earlier stops remain distinct. The wrapper rejects an outer BP below `044A` or nonempty inherited sessions before mutation; it inherits the earlier frame and alias assumptions. The stopped loader body and subsequent COMPARE logic are not executed.

The [COMPARE raw caller evidence](U1C_COMPARE_0126_CPU.md) pins the prologue and loaded call frame but explicitly does not grant readiness or caller unlock. The [saved-slot gate audit](SLOT_A_UP_ECL_GATE.md) statically lists COMPARE as its sixth operation. This fixture's preceding five SAVE operations are synthetic, so it does not establish the native saved-slot route. Synthetic checks cover sixth COMPARE entry and frame, standalone success, stack/data alias, malformed far call and stub, and insufficient-stack atomic rejection, plus inherited nested-state guards. No native differential, completed COMPARE, readiness, strict-credit or full-gameplay claim is made.
