## Behaviour

**Frame.** The routine is entered by a far call. `[SP+4]` is the RESULT far pointer, and `[SP+8]` is the ITEM far
pointer, which points to a 63-byte (3Fh) record. DS:[5D92] is the far pointer to the active character record. The
character's `+C8h/+CAh` holds the head of its list of 63-byte records, each linked at `+2Ah/+2Ch`.

1. **Capacity check.** 275B is called with the character and ITEM, reached through overlay stub `0085:004D`, whose
   loaded `JMP FAR` is a declared substitution checked as `RESOLVE`.
   - If AL is non-zero, the 10-character literal at `CS:022E` is loaded into a local by `0AF8:0B0B` (StrLoad) and
     displayed by `00BA:1693`. Then `*RESULT := 1` and the routine returns, with no append and no `0BB8`.
2. **Append.** Otherwise `*RESULT := 0`.
   - **Empty list:** `GetMem(3Fh)`, `Move(ITEM, node, 3Fh)`, `node.next := nil`, `head := node`.
   - **Non-empty list:** walk to the tail, then `GetMem`, `Move`, `new.next := nil`, `tail.next := new`.
3. **Finish.** `00BA:0BB8` is called on the character, then `RETF 8`.

**Statuses.**

- `OK`.
- `ARGUMENT`.
- `CODE`: a call site isn't in its original form.
- `RESOLVE`: the stub doesn't resolve to 275B.
- `CALLEE`: a composed callee stopped; the diagnostic gives the call index and the callee's raw status.
- `TRANSFER`.
- `LIMIT`: the list walk took more backward jumps than `walk_limit`.

The full contract is in `overlay_u23_append_0239_cpu.h`.

**Composed callees.** Every callee runs through its merged model:

- the 275B capacity check (`overlay_0077_275b_cpu`, composing 0BB8 and 13AB);
- StrLoad `0B0B`;
- display `00BA:1693`, with the EGA;
- GetMem `023F`, with its heap walk;
- Move `0A3F`;
- `00BA:0BB8`.
